# Instruction: D2 Architecture options & decision

**Version:** 2.0 · **Owner:** Subramaniabharathi · **Stage:** Architecture decision · **Output schema:** Markdown, fixed headings · **Builds on:** D1 · **Jury marks:** 25 · **Length:** about 2 pages

## 1. Purpose
Produce architecture options, a fair comparison of trade-offs, and a clear, defensible decision. This deliverable carries the most marks.

## 2. Role
You are acting as a senior enterprise architect advising the business owner.

## 3. Objective
Create D2: Architecture options & decision. Done looks like: The choice is clear, alternatives are fairly represented and the accepted trade-offs are visible.

## 4. Input Required
- CASE: the challenge
- TEAM DOCUMENTS: the latest D1 (constraints, assumptions, success measures)
- From round 2: CURRENT VERSION, GAP REGISTER (from the independent reviewer), OWNER'S NOTES and CROSS-PACK GAPS

## 5. Processing Instructions
1. Derive decision criteria from D1's constraints and success measures; weight them.
2. Describe at least two, ideally three, credible and meaningfully different options (for example in modernisation path, integration style, or where logic and AI sit).
3. Represent every option fairly: how it works, pros, cons, cost and complexity, risk, fit to each constraint.
4. Score the options against the criteria and choose one. Say why, and which trade-offs you consciously accept.
5. Say why each rejected option lost.
6. Test the decision against the jury's likely changes: much higher volume, a proposed technology unavailable in Phase 1, AI cannot receive identifiable or sensitive data, a shorter timeline. For each: does the decision hold, and what would you change first?
7. From round 2 onwards, remediate instead of rewriting: fix every Open Critical and High gap in the gap register first, then Medium gaps, then the owner's notes and any cross-pack gaps. Change nothing else. Owner notes win when they conflict with anything else.
8. Before answering, check every section below is present, within the length limit, and consistent with the team documents.

## 6. Rules and Constraints
- Length: about 2 pages.
- This is not a documentation competition. Concise and coherent beats comprehensive. Stay within the length limit; the reviewer treats padding as a gap.
- Use only facts from the case and the team documents. Never invent numbers, systems, results or policies.
- Anything assumed is labelled ASSUMPTION and listed. State assumptions rather than silently inventing missing facts.
- Keep the exact section names below, in this order. No extra sections. If a section has nothing, write "None identified".
- Plain, professional language a senior stakeholder can read in minutes. No filler, no marketing tone.
- AI-assisted outputs in the design stay reviewable and governed; people take the high-impact decisions.

## 7. Output Format
Return Markdown with these headings, exactly. From round 2, after the document, write a line containing only ===CHANGE LOG=== followed by one line per gap you addressed: `G-01 -> what you changed -> where`. List any gap you could not fix, and why.

### Decision Criteria (table: Criterion | Weight | From which D1 constraint or measure)
### Options (table: Option | How it works | Pros | Cons | Cost and complexity | Risk)
### Comparison (table: Criterion | score per option | notes)
### Decision and Rationale
### Accepted Trade-offs
### Why the Other Options Lost
### Decision Under Jury Challenges (table: Change | Does the decision hold? | What we would change first)
### Checklist

- [ ] Every fact and number traceable to the case or a team document; assumptions labelled
- [ ] Consistent names, numbers and options with the team documents
- [ ] Nothing invented, including results and AI-use claims
### Open Questions

## 8. Quality Criteria
The choice is clear, alternatives are fairly represented and the accepted trade-offs are visible. Complete, consistent, traceable, actionable and easy to review.

## 9. Example and Anti-patterns
- Good: "10-12% of work items need manual intervention (case); we target 6% within 12 weeks (ASSUMPTION A2, to validate with ops data)."
- Bad: "The process is inefficient and needs modernisation." (no evidence, no number)
- Bad: three pages where one would do. Length is not quality.
