# QA scorecard (AI Friday Quick Guide, section 04)

Score each dimension 0-100. The weighted total is calculated by the kit.

| Quality dimension | Reviewer checks | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage | All required elements of the brief and the deliverable are addressed | 25% |
| Completeness | Stakeholders, assumptions, NFRs, dependencies, risks, controls are not missing | 20% |
| Consistency | Aligns with the other deliverables; no contradictions | 15% |
| Evidence & traceability | Claims trace to source facts, labelled assumptions or test evidence | 15% |
| Business & technical quality | Feasible, secure, resilient, operable, tied to value | 15% |
| Clarity & decision readiness | A senior reader understands the decision, trade-offs and next steps | 10% |

## Severity scale for gaps
| Severity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | Breaks a stated constraint or makes the recommendation invalid. Must fix. |
| High | Material gap a senior reviewer would challenge. Fix before submission. |
| Medium | Weakens quality or clarity. Fix if time allows. |
| Low | Polish or wording. Optional. |

## Jury evaluation (AI Friday for Seniors workbook, section 11)
| Evaluation area | Marks | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem understanding and success measures | 15 | D1 |
| Architecture options, trade-offs and decision | 25 | D2 |
| Target-state architecture | 20 | D3 |
| POC / validation relevance and learning | 20 | D4 |
| Roadmap, risks and practical use of AI | 15 | D5 |
| Clarity of final presentation and jury response | 5 | Pitch |

**Jury principle:** reward clear reasoning, useful evidence and practical trade-offs. More documentation, more tools or a larger POC should not automatically score higher. The reviewer therefore treats padding and content beyond the length limit as a gap under Clarity & decision readiness.
