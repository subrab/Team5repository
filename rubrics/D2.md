# D2 Architecture options & decision: jury lens

**Jury marks:** 25 of 100 · **Length:** about 2 pages

**What done looks like (workbook):** The choice is clear, alternatives are fairly represented and the accepted trade-offs are visible.

The reviewer also checks that:
- Derive decision criteria from D1's constraints and success measures; weight them.
- Describe at least two, ideally three, credible and meaningfully different options (for example in modernisation path, integration style, or where logic and AI sit).
- Represent every option fairly: how it works, pros, cons, cost and complexity, risk, fit to each constraint.
- Score the options against the criteria and choose one. Say why, and which trade-offs you consciously accept.
- Say why each rejected option lost.
- Test the decision against the jury's likely changes: much higher volume, a proposed technology unavailable in Phase 1, AI cannot receive identifiable or sensitive data, a shorter timeline. For each: does the decision hold, and what would you change first?
