# D5 Delivery & risk plan: jury lens

**Jury marks:** 15 of 100 · **Length:** about 1.5 pages

**What done looks like (workbook):** The next steps are realistic, major risks are visible and the team can explain how AI influenced the work.

The reviewer also checks that:
- Plan Now (the first usable capability in about 12 weeks), Next and Later, with outcomes and dependencies, consistent with D2 and D3.
- Show how normal business processing continues during each step.
- List the top 5 risks, using D4's 'what remains unproven' where relevant, each with a mitigation and an owner.
- Link the roadmap to D1's success measures: which measure each horizon moves.
- Write a short AI-use reflection: one place AI accelerated the work and one place the team corrected, rejected or improved AI output. Use only the team human-corrections log; invent nothing.
