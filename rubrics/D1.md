# D1 Problem & success snapshot: jury lens

**Jury marks:** 15 of 100 · **Length:** about 1 page

**What done looks like (workbook):** A senior stakeholder can understand the problem and what success means in a few minutes.

The reviewer also checks that:
- Read the full case. Note every fact, number, constraint and target outcome it states.
- If the case invites a domain lens, choose one and say what a 'work item' is in it.
- Write the problem in 3-4 sentences: who is affected, what is broken, why it matters now.
- Pick the key constraints and say what each means for the solution.
- State 3-5 assumptions that fill real gaps in the case, each with how to validate it.
- Define 4-6 success measures, each with a baseline (from the case or ASSUMPTION), a target and how it is measured.
